Planning notes for your wedding

How far in advance should we order?

We recommend 10–14 days for wedding cakes. Larger or more detailed orders may need more time, so message us as early as you can with your date.

Can we mix a wedding cake with cupcakes or mini cakes?

Yes. Many couples choose a smaller wedding cake for the cutting moment and cupcakes or mini cakes for guests. Tell us your guest count in the enquiry and we will help you plan quantities.

Are the website figures the final price?

No. They are provisional guidance from the business. Your final quote depends on design, size, decorations and requirements, and is confirmed directly on WhatsApp before you order.

Can you match a design we found online?

Share a reference link or photo and we will tell you what is possible for your date, size and budget before you commit.
